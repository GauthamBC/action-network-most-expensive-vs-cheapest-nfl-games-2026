Key Findings

The Biggest NFL Stadium Cost Takeaways

Five quick takeaways from Action Network's 2026 ranking of NFL stadium costs for families.

$823.13
Key Finding 01

Huntington Bank Field Is the Cheapest NFL Stadium for Families

Huntington Bank Field, home of the Cleveland Browns, has the NFL’s lowest family-of-four stadium cost at $823.13, which is $651.42 below the league average.

$2,193.95
Key Finding 02

Highmark Stadium Is the Most Expensive

Highmark Stadium, home of the Buffalo Bills, tops the ranking at $2,193.95 for a family of four, $719.40 above the NFL average.

$1,474.55
Key Finding 03

The Average NFL Stadium Visit Costs $1,474.55 for a Family of Four

Across all 32 team-linked stadium entries, the average family-of-four package costs $1,474.55. Four tickets account for $1,352.76, with food, drinks and parking adding another $121.79.

$2,049.80
Key Finding 04

Soldier Field Has the NFL’s Most Expensive Tickets

A four-ticket package at Soldier Field costs $2,049.80, the highest in the NFL and $697.04 above the league-wide four-ticket average of $1,352.76.

$86.63
Key Finding 05

Allegiant Stadium Is the Most Expensive for Food and Drinks

A family buying two beers, two soft drinks and four hot dogs at Allegiant Stadium would spend $86.63, the highest combined food-and-drink cost in the NFL. The league average for the same concession bundle is $63.73.

How the Ranking Works

Methodology and Sources

Total Cost for a Family of Four

The ranking estimates what a family of four would pay for a regular-season NFL game in 2026. Each total includes five cost components, with child admission shown separately as supporting data.

Supporting DataChild AdmissionNot included in ranking

Ticket Pricing

Ticket Sample539,192 listingsAcross all 32 NFL teams and 272 Ticketmaster event pages.
Collection WindowJuly 27–August 2, 2026Ticket listings were captured during this seven-day research period.
Ticket TypesPrimary + Verified ResaleBoth listing types were included in the Ticketmaster sample.
Market ComparisonStubHub + SeatGeekComparison only to sense-check the Ticketmaster sample. Not included in the final rankings.

Ticket prices used in the ranking were collected from Ticketmaster. StubHub and SeatGeek were used only as secondary comparison points to help confirm that the Ticketmaster sample was broadly representative of the wider resale market.

Ultra-premium and luxury packages, along with unusually low promotional listings, were removed to better reflect regular-season ticket prices a family could realistically encounter.

Food, Drink and Parking

Where official 2026 stadium concession and parking prices were not yet available, 2025 prices from Fox 5, Dose and Team Marketing Report's Fan Cost Index were used as the baseline.

Inflation SourceU.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics CPILatest available 12-month Consumer Price Index changes, through June 2026.
Applied ToBeer, Soft Drinks, Hot Dogs + ParkingBLS CPI data was used to estimate 2026 costs for these items where official 2026 prices were not yet available.
Inflation adjustment — how it was used: 2025 prices for beers, soft drinks, hot dogs and parking were adjusted to estimated 2026 levels using category-specific Consumer Price Index changes published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
  • +3.1%BeerBeer, ale and other malt beverages away from home
  • +1.9%Soft DrinksCarbonated drinks
  • +7.2%Hot DogsFrankfurters
  • +3.8%ParkingParking fees and tolls

CPI adjustments were applied only to beer, soft drinks, hot dogs and parking costs. 2026 ticket prices came directly from ticket listings and were not inflation-adjusted.

Ranking Notes

The cheapest ranking orders teams from the lowest total to the highest, while the most expensive ranking reverses that order. The Jets and Giants, and the Rams and Chargers, remain separate because their ticket costs differ even though each pair shares a stadium. Child admission policies are shown as supporting information and do not alter the package total.
